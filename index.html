About

I work at the seam between machine learning research and software that people actually use.

Most of what I build starts as a question — can a language model reason over a police records database? can a drone track a person through a disaster site? can a finance app explain why it flagged your spending? — and ends as a deployed system with a backend, a UI, and a real user on the other side.

My core is Python and the modern ML stack: PyTorch, TensorFlow, OpenCV, and increasingly agentic architectures built on retrieval and tool use. Around that I write the rest of the product — FastAPI services, Next.js and Flutter frontends, and the IoT and robotics glue that connects software to hardware.

Two internships at Latrics India and Pantech Solutions taught me the unglamorous half: sprint planning, code review, documentation that survives handover. Exchange programmes in South Korea and China taught me the other half — how to build with people who don't share your first language.

Currently

  • Building Meika, an explainable AI personal-finance assistant
  • Exploring fully-local agentic systems that run without cloud inference
